Our ingredients

Selected, fresh and genuine

At Scrocchia 80, the quality of our pizza bases is our priority. We pride ourselves on using only selected, fresh and genuine ingredients to ensure a final product that satisfies everyone’s palate.

  1. 1

    Flours

    We use a high-quality, stone-ground flour to give our pizza base a perfect consistency and authentic flavor. The selection of soft wheat guarantees optimal leavening and a crispy crust.

  2. 2

    Water

    Water is a fundamental ingredient for the dough. We choose pure, contaminant-free water, which helps to obtain a soft consistency and uniform leavening.

  3. 3

    Natural yeast

    We use sourdough to give our pizza bases a unique flavor and superior digestibility. The natural yeast promotes slow fermentation, developing unmistakable aromas and scents.

  4. 4

    Sea salt

    We add sea salt to enhance the flavor of the ingredients and improve the structure of the dough. Salt is essential for proper leavening and to balance the flavors.

  5. 5

    Sunflower oil

    A neutral and stable oil that ensures optimal crispness and a uniform golden colour during baking.

  6. 6

    Natural ingredients and no preservatives

    We are committed to offering products free from preservatives and chemical additives. All our ingredients are natural and selected to guarantee an authentic gastronomic experience.

The combination of these ingredients, worked with passion and attention, allows us to create pizza bases that are not only delicious, but also healthy. At Scrocchia 80, every bite tells the story of tradition and love for genuine cuisine.
